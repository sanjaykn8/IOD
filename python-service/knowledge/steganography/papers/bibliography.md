# Consolidated Bibliography

All citations referenced across `algorithms/*.md`, gathered in one
place. These are offered as representative literature for each
classical technique family, consistent with well-established
steganography survey citations -- they are not independently re-
verified page-by-page by this project, and should be treated as
pointers for further reading rather than an audited systematic review.

1. C.K. Chan and L.M. Cheng, "Hiding data in images by simple LSB
   substitution," *Pattern Recognition*, 37(3):469-474, 2004.
2. J. Fridrich, M. Goljan, R. Du, "Detecting LSB steganography in
   color and gray-scale images," *IEEE MultiMedia*, 8(4):22-28, 2001.
3. D.C. Wu and W.H. Tsai, "A steganographic method for images by
   pixel-value differencing," *Pattern Recognition Letters*,
   24(9-10):1613-1626, 2003.
4. H.C. Wu, N.I. Wu, C.S. Tsai, M.S. Hwang, "Image steganographic
   scheme based on pixel-value differencing and LSB replacement
   methods," *IEE Proceedings - Vision, Image and Signal Processing*,
   152(5), 2005.
5. A. Westfeld, "F5 -- A Steganographic Algorithm," *Information
   Hiding 2001*, LNCS vol. 2137, Springer, 2001.
6. N.F. Johnson and S. Jajodia, "Exploring steganography: Seeing the
   unseen," *IEEE Computer*, 31(2):26-34, 1998.
7. P.Y. Chen and H.J. Lin, "A DWT Based Approach for Image
   Steganography," *International Journal of Applied Science and
   Engineering*, 4(3):275-290, 2006.
8. C. Bergman and J. Davidson, "Unitary embedding for data hiding with
   the SVD," *Proc. SPIE 5681, Security, Steganography, and
   Watermarking of Multimedia Contents VII*, 2005.
9. R. Liu and T. Tan, "An SVD-based watermarking scheme for protecting
   rightful ownership," *IEEE Transactions on Multimedia*, 4(1):121-128,
   2002.
